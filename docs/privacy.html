Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Sleeps is a countdown app for kids. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We never see your countdowns — they belong to you.

The short version

We collect nothing

Sleeps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information — not from grown-ups and not from children. There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile.

No ads, no analytics, no trackers

There are no advertisements, no third-party analytics, and no tracking or advertising SDKs in the app. Nothing about how you use Sleeps is measured or sent anywhere.

Your countdowns stay yours

Every countdown you create is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your countdowns also sync across your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud, using a private database tied to your Apple Account.

That data lives in your personal iCloud. The developer has no server that receives it and no ability to read it.

Reminders are on-device

If you turn on reminders for a countdown, the notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server and nothing about your reminders leaves the device.

Made for kids

Sleeps is designed for children and follows Apple's Kids Category guidelines and children's privacy laws, including COPPA and GDPR-K.

  • No personal information is collected from anyone, including children.
  • No behavioral advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • The only links that leave the app — this Privacy Policy and Support — sit behind a parental gate so a grown-up opens them.

The details

Data we collect

None. On Apple's privacy questionnaire, Sleeps is labeled “Data Not Collected.”

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, whose iCloud service optionally syncs your countdowns between your own devices under Apple's Privacy Policy. No other third parties receive any data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
